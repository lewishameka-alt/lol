# Surveillance / stakeout craft (Lewis)

What made earlier batches fail — and what to do instead.

## Failures to avoid
- Subject large / centred / facing the lens (looks posed)
- Identical “twin” biker NPCs
- Same white tee + shades costume every frame
- Heavy fake film-scratch overlays and neon Hollywood night grade
- Theatrical full steering-wheel framing every time
- Readable club names / logos on patches
- Beauty-sharp close-ups sold as “distance”

## Required craft
- **Distance:** Lewis is small-to-medium in frame (roughly 15–35% of frame height). Shot from ~30–80m.
- **Awareness:** Never looking at camera. Walking away, side profile, three-quarter back, head turned to conversation.
- **Optic:** Long telephoto compression, slight atmospheric haze, mild focus softness on distant faces — NOT beauty sharpness.
- **Vehicle POV:** Subtle — dirty glass, a thin A-pillar, mirror edge, OR faint dash reflection. Do not repeat giant steering-wheel silhouettes every shot. Mix: some pure long-lens street (no car interior).
- **Texture:** Natural digital noise / mild grain only. No scratchy found-footage filters.
- **Lewis locks:** Match refs; black sunglasses on face always; vary clothes (charcoal hoodie, work shirt, flannel, navy tee, bomber, faded black jacket).
- **Bikers:** Distinct ages/builds/faces; worn leather; generic patches with NO readable real gang names; cruiser/chopper bikes as environment.
- **Light:** Boring real light — flat overcast, harsh noon, weak streetlight, early blue hour. Avoid cinematic teal-orange and sodium “movie night.”
- **Composition:** Leave headroom and empty road/sky; subject off-centre; environment must dominate the frame.

## Prompt skeleton (use every time)
1. Opening: “WIDE establishing / wildlife-telephoto / CCTV-range surveillance still — NOT a portrait.” Prefer **16:9**.
2. Hard scale lock (models ignore soft wording): “CRITICAL SCALE: subject under 18–22% of frame height; environment dominates 80%+.”
3. Awareness lock: “Back fully turned / profile / three-quarter into scene — NEVER looking at camera, never looking back over shoulder.”
4. Lewis identity + sunglasses ON + **unique outfit** this frame.
5. Unique location + unique secondary people (distinct ages/builds — name their differences).
6. Subtle POV cue (one of: dusty windshield / thin A-pillar / mirror tip / rainy glass / none). Never giant steering wheel.
7. Boring light + mild digital zoom noise.
8. Negatives: movie still, cinematic grade, portrait/medium close-up, looking at camera, beauty lighting, plastic skin, twin extras, costume cosplay, watermark, text, readable gang names.

## What actually worked (batch-006)
- 16:9 + “WIDE” + numeric % of frame height beats “30–80 metres” alone.
- Settings refs for place texture + Lewis refs for identity.
- Cull anything where he fills the frame or looks at the lens — regenerate, don’t ship.

## Negative look
movie still, cinematic color grade, portrait framing, subject looking at camera, beauty lighting, plastic skin, identical twin extras, costume cosplay bikers, watermark, text overlay, found-footage scratches, giant steering wheel every frame
