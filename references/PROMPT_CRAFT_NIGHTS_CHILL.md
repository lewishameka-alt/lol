# Nights-out + chill candid craft (Lewis)

What made batches 007–008 fail — and what to do instead.

## Failures to avoid
- **Setting loop:** same pub booth / beer garden / carpark / couch recycled every 20 frames
- **AI mates:** synced fake smiles, model faces, plastic skin, identical “cast extra” look
- **Clean venues:** glossy club stock-photo backgrounds
- **Group portrait energy:** everyone facing lens with the same grin
- Ignoring `references/settings/` for place texture

## Required craft — settings
- **One unique location per shot.** Never reuse a title/setting in the same batch.
- Hyper-specific regional Victoria detail (name the mess): sticky table, scratched vinyl, fluoro buzz, Coles bag, muddy ute tray, peeling paint, empty schooner, etc.
- Environment must read as a real phone snap location, not a set.
- Pull vibe/texture from `references/settings/` — never copy people from those refs.

## Required craft — mates (nights-out)
- Invented strangers only — never recreate Lewis’s real mates from refs.
- **Distinct humans:** different ages (18–45), builds, hair, skin tones common in regional Vic.
- **Anti-AI face lock:** slight facial asymmetry, visible pores, uneven stubble, tired eyes, mid-sentence or mid-laugh mouth (not a posed grin), imperfect teeth, one person mid-blink OK.
- **Anti-sync:** do NOT give every person the same smile angle; one looking away, one talking, one bored.
- Clothes: real thrift/pub gear, not matching outfits.
- Prefer 1–2 mates over a crowded “Instagram group” unless the scene needs more.

## Required craft — Lewis
- Identity from `references/lewis/` — looking good is the lock to keep.
- Sunglasses ON face every frame.
- Vary outfits every shot (no white-tee loop).

## Prompt skeleton
1. Lewis identity + sunglasses HARD LOCK.
2. Unique one-off setting with concrete messy details.
3. If mates: named differences + anti-AI face/expression lock + anti-sync.
4. Phone photo physics: flash OR real night noise OR boring daylight — not cinema grade.
5. Negatives: synced fake smiles, model extras, plastic skin, beauty filter, stock-photo venue, recycled generic “pub booth”, watermark, text.

## Negative look
AI stock group, synced grins, plastic skin, beauty filter, cinematic teal-orange, identical twin extras, glossy club brochure venue, watermark, text overlay
